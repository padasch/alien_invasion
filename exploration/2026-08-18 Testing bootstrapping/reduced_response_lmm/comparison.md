# Reduced-form treatment effects versus former SEM totals

The reduced-form models completed 1,000 successful block-stratified
container-cluster bootstrap refits for all 12 estimable species-response
combinations (12,000 successful refits; no failed attempts). The two Quercus
senescence combinations remain unavailable because the source analyses contain
no usable response observations.

The directly fitted treatment coefficients closely reproduce the former
path-summed SEM totals:

- all 48 available treatment-effect directions agree;
- the maximum absolute point-estimate difference is 0.005 standardized units;
- 47 of 48 bootstrap significance classifications agree;
- Quercus vitality under mixed culture is the only changed classification
  (reduced form: estimate 0.135, bootstrap P = 0.066; former path sum:
  estimate 0.135, bootstrap P = 0.044).

Figure 6 now uses the directly fitted reduced-form coefficients. This avoids
presenting an algebraic SEM path sum as the primary treatment estimate while
preserving the substantive treatment pattern.

Converged singular fits were retained and audited. They occurred in 2,791 of
12,000 bootstrap refits, concentrated in Quercus total volume (701), Fagus
volume increment (596), Fagus vitality (513), and Fagus total volume (420).
The Quercus total-volume point model was singular; the other estimable point
models were nonsingular. These boundary estimates concern random-effect
variance components and do not by themselves invalidate the fixed treatment
contrasts, but the affected endpoints should be interpreted with appropriate
caution.
